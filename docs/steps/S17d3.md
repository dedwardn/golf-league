# S17d3 Admin round state

**Implemented**

- A stored round's screen has a state card under its header: the state as a pill beside a time. A draft shows its Opens, an open round its Closes, and a closed round the time it closed.
- A draft's Open round is a link to `/admin/rounds/{n}/open`. That page stores nothing and asks "Open round {n} now? Players can hand in cards and the setup is fixed for good.", with the stored setup in one line, "Yes, open round {n}" and Cancel. The round card is hidden while it asks (the owner, at the plan).
- An open round has Close round, which records `utc_now()` as `closed_at`. A closed round has Undo close, or "A round with a late card stays closed." when `may_reopen` says no. Each action is one tap.
- A post the state does not allow (a second tap, a stale page, round 99) stores nothing and opens the round's page as it now stands. No refusal is worded.

**Where**

- `src/league/web/admin_round.py`: `_round_screen` gains `asking` and passes `reopens`, read through `may_reopen`. The GET `_ask_to_open` and the POSTs `_open`, `_close` and `_reopen` each check the admin cookie, call their use case with a refusal suppressed, and open the round's page.
- `src/league/web/templates/admin-round.html`: the state card, the asking card, and the round card hidden while asking. The stale S17d3 note now names S17e alone.
- `tests/web/test_admin_round.py`:

  - Yes, Close round at a fixed `NOW`, Undo close, and the late card;
  - the nine disallowed state and action pairs, round 99 included, and the ask on a round that is no draft;
  - a new round's form without the card;
  - the cookie refusal extended to the four new routes, each on a round where it would succeed.
- Docs:
  - Commit `12881eb`: BUILD_PLAN's S17d3 section settled with its Accept, the S21 section, and SPEC's Round and formats row.
  - With this step: ARCHITECTURE's `web/admin_round.py` row, BUILD_PLAN marks S17d3 done, and `docs/design/admin-round.html`'s state card is redrawn with its form, its asking card drawn beside it.

**Public API**

- `league.web.admin_round.round_router() -> APIRouter`: adds GET and POST `/admin/rounds/{number}/open`, and POST `/admin/rounds/{number}/close` and `/admin/rounds/{number}/reopen`. Nothing else public changed.

**Deliberately not done**

- Cards, correct, void, Enter card and manual awards are S17e's.
- What a draft's state card reads in an individual season, with no format in its setup line or while a duel's pairings are unpublished, is S21's. A draft without a format can only be stored by calling `create_round` directly. Its asking page would fail on the format, and no screen can store such a draft before S21.

**The audit**

The reviewer raised seven findings, and the architect ruled on them:
- **1, applied to this report, and answered by the owner.** The shots of a draft and its asking card are missing, so Deviations now says so. The owner took option (b): their phone click-through stands in for the two shots.
- **2, declined.** Folding the state pill shared with Rounds into a macro would add a non-screen template, two imports and an ARCHITECTURE clause, and remove one expression from each template (precedents: S17d2a finding 4, S16c2 finding 7).
- **3, declined.** A `_draft` helper moves the two-line draft guard without shortening either caller.
- **4, applied.** The empty `{% if asking %}` branch is gone. The round card reads `{% if typed %} ... {% elif not asking %} ... {% endif %}`, guarded by the asking test's `FORM` and `fields` assertions.
- **5, declined.** The fixed `NOW` on the not-allowed matrix is plan ruling 10. It makes a wrong close that re-records `closed_at` change the store in a known way.
- **6, declined.** `card--mine` is only on the state card's section, so its absence says no card is drawn. `state(...) == []` would only miss the pill row.
- **7, recorded, no change.** Open's suppressed refusal of unpublished pairings, and the asking page's format for a draft without one, are carried by S21's section of BUILD_PLAN. Before S21 no screen can store either draft.
