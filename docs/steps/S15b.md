# S15b Standings for the viewer

**Implemented**

- Every row on the standings screen shows its counted cards, as `N rounds`.
- The viewer's row is marked, reads "You" in an individual season and the team's name in a team season, and shows its round totals with dropped ones struck through and the gap to the place above.
- A table with a `best_n` shows the best N line to a viewer with a row.
- Each closed round's winners show below the table, newest first, with "70 net" or in a team season "3.0 points", on every tab, for the rounds R12 shows the viewer.
- The team branch moved from `season.py` to `team_rounds.py`, as T3's open question ruled, in a commit of its own.
