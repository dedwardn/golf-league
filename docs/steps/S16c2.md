# S16c2 Getting around and notices

**Implemented**

- **The notice screen.** Every refusal a player meets shows `notice.html`, a message and a Home button, with its status unchanged. "No such round." now reads "That round isn't open.", and a visit without a valid link cookie reads "Open your personal link from the group chat."
- **A card that is in.** Register card and Check the card open the round page (303) once the viewer's card, or their team's team card, is in, in an open round or a closed one. Submit card then reads "Your card for round N is already in. Ask the admin if it needs a correction." (the owner, at the plan).
- **Double tap and Back.** The card screen's script disables Submit card once the form is sent. Check the card is a GET on `/rounds/{number}/cards/new` with the 18 scores in the query. The card screens are sent with `Cache-Control: no-store`, and the script reloads a screen the browser restores from its back-forward cache, so Back after Submit card lands on the round page (the owner, at the click-through).
- **Getting around.** Each open round's card on home links its heading to `/rounds/{number}`. A Round tab at `/round` sits between Home and Standings on home, standings and both round pages. It opens the oldest open round's page, else the latest closed round's, and in a season with neither it shows the notice "No round is open right now." with status 404
