# S11 Store, read side

**Implemented**

SQLite tables for the facts `compute_season` reads, built by one Alembic migration that
`migrate` runs on a connection it is handed. `load_season_facts` turns one season's rows into
`SeasonFacts`. It leaves out draft rounds, voided cards and voided team cards, and validates
every JSON column through pydantic into the domain dataclasses, whose own checks then run. Keys
and partial unique indexes hold R13's uniqueness rules: a card id and a round number are unique
in their season, and a round holds one counted card per player and one counted team card per team.

265 tests, 23 of them new, one of them in the domain. 100 percent coverage of `league.domain` and `league.store`. Alembic
loads the migration files itself, so coverage does not measure them, but every test runs them.
Before this report, each of these bugs was seeded into `load.py` and failed at least one test:
- voided cards kept
- draft rounds kept
- manual awards ordered by competitor
- `late_until` dropped
- provisional flags dropped
- another season's cards read
- points passed through a float.

Dropping `order_by(rounds.c.number)` fails no test. SQLite reads a season's rounds through the
primary key index, so they come back in number order whatever order they were inserted in. The
`order_by` stays, because that ordering is SQLite's query plan, not a guarantee. The plan's reversed insertion of rounds (architect ruling 11) was dropped at audit for the same reason.

**Deviations from plan**

- Pyright's `extraPaths` is `["src", "tests"]`, not the `["tests"]` the owner asked for. Once
  `extraPaths` is set, pyright no longer finds `league` under `src`. `["tests"]` alone gave 39
  `reportMissingTypeStubs` errors on the domain's own imports.
- The row writers sit in `test_schema.py`, and `test_load.py` imports them, rather than a private
  helper in `test_load.py`. The schema tests need the same rows, and a shared module would have
  been a file outside the plan.
- About 790 new lines rather than about 550. The two tests the architect added and the owner's
  counted-card index account for most of the difference, and the migration is 89 lines.
- Two additions approved with the plan: the partial unique indexes on counted cards and their
  tests (owner, 4), and naive UTC as settled (owner, 5).
- `facts.py` and `tests/domain/test_facts.py`, outside the plan's files, changed at audit at the
  owner's word (finding 1).

**Open questions**

Answered by this step:
- Time zones (owner, 5): datetimes are naive UTC in the domain and the store. Services convert
  the clock to UTC, and the web layer shows local time. Settled, not carried to S13.
- S2's storing of the `provisional` flag: the `season_players.provisional` column.
- S9b's note that a primary key on card id makes card ids unique in a season: the primary key on
  `(season_id, card_id)`.
- S5's remark that config arrives from JSON at S11. `__post_init__` runs on load, so
  `FieldConfig`'s refusal of a field ranked on holes now runs on stored config.
- S9b's carried item 2 was already closed by T3, where R9 requires a manual award to name a
  competitor of the season.

Carried to S14:
- A round's `state` and `closed_at` are separate columns, and nothing ties them together.
  `load_season_facts` reads `state` only to leave out draft rounds and takes `closed_at` as the
  truth for open or closed. Whether the database should require `state = 'closed'` exactly when
  `closed_at` is set depends on SPEC open decision 7 (whether `closed_at` survives a reopen).
  Settle it with S14's close and reopen use cases.

**Audit**

The reviewer made 12 findings; the architect ruled on each.

1. The CHECK on season mode is a bound on a fact held by the store, against ARCHITECTURE's store
   map. Sent to the owner, who chose option A: `SeasonFacts.mode` is a `str` checked in
   `__post_init__`, as `FieldConfig.basis` is checked at run time (S5), with
   `test_r15_a_mode_outside_individual_and_team_is_an_error` in `test_facts.py`. The CHECK is
   gone from `schema.py` and the migration, and so is its refusal case in `test_schema.py`. A
   stored mode outside the two now fails on load, with a ValueError. The CHECKs on round state
   and a card's holder stay, since those rules have no domain home. Also applied: `load.py`'s
   docstring now says loading adds no bound of its own, and the ARCHITECTURE store map names
   the two remaining CHECKs.
2. A round's `state` and `closed_at` can disagree. Declined for S11, carried to S14 above.
3. `compare_metadata` does not see CHECKs or partial index clauses. Applied: the drift test
   compares every table's and index's SQL from the migrated database with `metadata.create_all`
   on a second one. The text matches exactly. A changed CHECK or `sqlite_where` in `schema.py`
   fails it.
4. No test for R13's card id and round number unique in a season. Applied: two refusal cases,
   each built so only the primary key can refuse it.
5. The discriminators were untested. Applied: a contest without its `kind` and a format without
   its `format` are refused, matching "discriminator". Dropping either `Field(discriminator=...)`
   fails its case.
6. The `database` fixture is defined in both test files. Declined: a `tests/store/conftest.py`
   clashes with `tests/domain/conftest.py` as the module `conftest`, and importing a fixture
   needs a lint suppression. S12 replaces the row writers.
7. `test_load.py` spelled out what `_round` builds. Applied.
8. `_create_cards` was used once. Applied: inlined into `upgrade`. The loop over player and team
   for the two indexes stays, since "spelled out" means not built from the tables, and the drift
   test checks the index names and clauses.
9. Reversed round insertion claimed coverage it did not give. Applied: rounds are inserted in
   order.
10. Two test names did not cite a rule. Applied: `test_r13_draft_round_is_left_out` and
    `test_r5_r9_r10_r16_config_out_of_bounds_or_untagged_is_refused_on_load`, over the list
    `REFUSED_CONFIGS`.
11. `_tee` was a single-use wrapper. Applied: inlined.
12. The report left out `a_card`. Applied, with the line counts corrected.
