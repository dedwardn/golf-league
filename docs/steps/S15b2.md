# S15b2 Standings round grid

**Implemented**

- The viewer's row shows a grid with one cell per closed round of the table, in round order, each under "R1", "R2" and so on, with the round total.
- A dropped round is struck through, and a round without a round total shows a dash.
- A segment tab's grid shows that segment's closed rounds. A round R12 hides keeps its cell: only its winners are hidden.
- The gap is the difference between the two rows' points as shown, so gold reads "6.7 to 1st".
