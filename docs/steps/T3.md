# T3 Team season pipeline

**Implemented**

A season has a `mode`. In a team season `compute_season` plays every card and team card off the
seed index. It builds each team's R17 score per contest and wraps it in a `NetEntry` whose
competitor is the team, timed by its latest card. It runs field and allplay over those entries
and makes one table row per team. A closed round's winners are the teams with the highest round
total. `SeasonFacts` refuses a team season that breaks R15's shape.

*The golden team season.* Tee A has rating equal to par, slope 113 and stroke index equal to
hole number. So a course handicap is the index, a playing handicap at an allowance is that share
rounded half up, and strokes fall on holes 1 to H. Cards are par except the listed holes, each
one over, so net is 72 plus the overs less H. The exceptions are hal's round-2 hole 18, a 9 on a
par 4, and gold's round-5 hole 18, an 8. Teams and seeds: red = ann 2.0, bob 10.0; blue = cam
4.0, dee 16.0; green = eve 6.0, fay 12.0; gold = gus 8.0, hal 18.0. Every round closes, best 4
count, and every allplay has scale 3.

In a team season `SeasonFacts` raises `ValueError` for any of these (R15):
- a seeded player in no team
- a team of other than two players
- a round without a format
- a contest other than field or allplay.

In either mode it raises `ValueError` for a manual award that names no competitor of the season:
in an individual season a seeded player, in a team season a team (R9, the owner's ruling on audit
finding 5).

`compute_season`'s signature is unchanged. In a team season:
- Rounds stay in `facts.rounds` order. A round's awards are its contests' in config order, each
  in team id order, then its manual awards.
- A seed index missing for a card it reads is a `KeyError`.

**Deviations from plan**

1. The private helpers in `season.py` differ from the plan's list:
   - `_team_round` became `_team_rounds`, over the whole season.
   - `_team_entry` calls `_own_ball` or `_shared_ball`, one per kind of format.
   - `_team_contest` builds one contest's `ContestEntries`.
   
   `_team_contest` takes the season's facts rather than the team members and seeds, and works
   out the members itself on each call. That keeps `season.py` at 298 lines, under the
   300-line limit, without moving code out of the files in the plan.
2. `TEAM_SIZE = 2` in `facts.py`, so the size check names its constant, as the module's other
   bounds do.
3. The team members are typed `tuple[str, ...]`, not a pair, so the dict built from the sorted
   mapping needs no unpacking. `SeasonFacts` has checked there are two.

**Deliberately not done**

- R13 acceptance and R12 visibility for team cards: S10.
- Locking a seed index once round one closes (R15, D17): access and services.
- Refusing player cards in a shared-ball round, team cards in an own-ball round, and a format or
  team cards in an individual season. D16 rules them out, but no rule makes them errors. The
  pipeline does not read them.
- Late cards in field and allplay: SPEC decision 4.
- A team season's screens: SPEC decision 9.

Answered here from T2's list:
- the contest allowance in a team season, which is not read
- wrapping a team score in a `NetEntry`
- a shared-ball team with no team card, which has no entry
- late filtering, by the entry's latest card.

Also answered: T1's team round winners, and S9b's carried item 2. A manual award for a player
with no seed index is now an error under R9, so it can no longer reach no table unnoticed.

**Audit**

1. The two golden fixtures share their tee, time helpers and card helper. Declined: each is a
   hand-computed fixture the owner signed off, read against its own step report. If T3 imported
   S9b's pieces, an edit to S9b's fixture would silently change T3's expected values.
2. `with_round` was a copy of `test_season.py`'s. Applied: it lives in `conftest.py` and both
   files import it.
3. `RoundView`'s docstring was wrong for an individual season, which has a winner from its
   on-time cards even when nobody holds an award. Applied: winners are empty for a closed round
   with no on-time card (R10) or, in a team season, no award from a contest (R15).
4. `compute_season` promised that a card from a player in no team is not read, which no rule
   decides and no test pinned. Applied: the promise is gone. The KeyError sentence now says a
   card it reads needs a seed index the facts lack.
5. Spec gaps in R15's shape checks. A team member with no seed index: declined. Teams and seeds
   are built by one caller, so the lookup's own KeyError stands (ARCHITECTURE, Errors). Three
   shapes go to the owner, since no rule makes them errors:
   - a manual award naming a player in a team season
   - a format or team cards in an individual season
   - player cards in a shared-ball round, or team cards in an own-ball round.
6. Duplication in `season.py`. The two netting expressions: declined. They read different
   indexes (R6's per card, and the seed at the format's allowance), and a helper adds lines to a
   file at its limit. Team ids through `_team_members`: declined. It moves the code without
   reducing it.
7. `_own_ball` returning early when a card is missing: declined. It would put R17's "both cards"
   rule into `season.py`, which `own_ball_score` already owns (T2).
8. Tests. The first assert of the late-team-card test repeating the golden winners: declined. It
   carries the test's claim, and the second is the control. `match="format"` was loose: applied,
   as `match="has no format"`.

The owner ruled on finding 5's three shapes: (c) plus (a). A manual award that names no
competitor loses points silently, so the owner made it an error in SPEC R9. `SeasonFacts` checks
it in `_check_manual_awards`, with two tests: an individual season's award for an unseeded player,
and a team season's award naming a player. The other shapes are harmless when ignored. The owner
added to S14's accept line in BUILD_PLAN that its use cases never create them.
