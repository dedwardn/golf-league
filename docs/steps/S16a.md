# S16a Card entry

**Implemented**

- **Register card on home.** In a team season, each open round on home has Register card beside Set up the bay. It shows while the round takes the viewer's card, and leaves once their card is in, or in a shared-ball round their team's card.
- **Typing a card.** Register card opens Check the card with 18 empty inputs, each labelled with its hole and par, under the round's course, tee name and par. Typing 2 to 9 moves to the next hole, and a 1 waits for its second digit.
- **Checking a card.** "Check the card" shows the screen again with the scores as typed.
  - Each hole that is empty, not a whole number or outside 1 to 19 is outlined, a hole that is not a whole number comes back empty, and one warn line names those holes.
  - With no hole in error, it shows out, in and total and enables Submit card. Any edit disables Submit card again until the next check.
- **Submitting.** "Submit card" hands the card in and lands on home. With a hole in error it shows the screen again with that hole outlined, and stores nothing.
- **The score bound.** R13's bound is now 1 to 19, on `Card`. First, in a commit of its own (`73830b6`), the home and bay views moved out of `results.py`.

  - `CardEntry` has no `format`.

  - **An S1 test's card.** `test_r3_net_is_gross_minus_allocated_strokes` used gross 3 to 20, and the new bound refuses 20. It now runs gross 2 to 19, with each net one lower and a total of 169. It still tests R3's two strokes on holes 1 and 10. It is not a golden fixture: their highest score is 9.

2. **Submit stores a card in an individual season.** Declined. R13 and R15 decide acceptance in both modes.
3. **Home asks `card_in` where entry asks `accepts_card`.** Declined. For a team member in an open round the two are equal.
