# S10 Access

**Implemented**

`domain/access.py` answers four yes/no questions from facts and a passed `now`. Does the round
take the card a seeded player hands in now (R13, R15)? Does a player see a round's cards and
results (R12)? Is a team season's seed index locked (R15)? Is a tee locked (R14)? In a
shared-ball round the card a player hands in, and the one that shows them the round, is their
team's team card. `RoundFacts` carries `late_until`.

**Deviations from plan**

None from the approved plan. Against the first draft, as ruled before approval:
- `accepts_card` takes `SeasonFacts` instead of `teams`, and refuses a player with no seed index
  (owner, option a). `test_r13_a_player_without_a_seed_index_is_refused` covers an individual

  round nor a voided card reaches the domain.
- R14's lock on a round's contest config and pairings once it opens: S14. Its reopen waits on
  SPEC decision 7.
- R14's rule that a duel round opens only once its pairings are published: S14, per S9b.

- What a player without a card sees, which is who has one: a view, S13 and S15.

  cases that create cards, S13's submit a card and S14's correct, each on its accept line. Here a
  player in a shared-ball round is asked about the team card by design. `accepts_card` cannot
  catch it, since in a shared-ball round it only asks about the team card.
