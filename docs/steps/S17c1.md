# S17c1 Create a season

**Implemented**

- Season setup at `/admin/season`, for the season `LEAGUE_SEASON` names, built from `admin-season.html`. The admin link now lands here.
- **Form, while the season isn't stored:** the name as text, Best rounds count, the 70% seed share as text, every stored player in id order with a Golfbox handicap input and the line "A player without a Golfbox handicap is not in the season.", one team row (a name and two players) for every two players on the roster, and bay lines with Add a line and a Remove (×) button per line.
- **Save season** stores a team season through `create_season`. Each seed index is 70 percent of the Golfbox handicap, half up, and a "+1.2" is a plus handicap. A refused save answers 400 with every field as typed and one warn card. A team season with no team is refused with "A season has at least one team."
- **Only a tap on Save season stores.** The form's first submit button, which Enter presses, is an invisible one (an inline style, out of the tab order) named `redraw`, so Enter in a field redraws the form as typed, like Add a line and Remove, and stores nothing. A stored season cannot be saved again, so an early Enter would have been final.
- **Read-back, once the season is stored:** no inputs. It shows best N (or "Every round"), each seeded player's league index and team in id order, the teams in team id order, and the bay lines as written. The seed share shows in a team season only.
- **Elsewhere:** a team season's seed index must be -5.0 to 54.0 (R15). `create_season` refuses a season id that is already stored. Season is first in the admin nav, Add a player has the back arrow to the season, and `scripts/demo.py serve --new-season` serves a season nobody stored, so the form can be clicked through.
- **Shots at 390 px:** the form, served by `scripts/demo.py serve --new-season`, has a scrollWidth equal to its clientWidth of 390, and the read-back's shot shows no horizontal scroll.
- **Click-through:** the owner clicked through the form and the read-back on the laptop and on the phone, and passed it. The Enter change came after it. I checked it in Chromium at 390 px through Playwright: Enter in a Golfbox field redrew the form with "8,6" kept, no warn card and nothing stored, and the invisible button measures 1 × 1 px. WebKit is not installed here, so Safari is unchecked.
