# S17b Admin players

**Implemented**

- Add a course and tee is rebuilt from the phone-first `admin-tee.html`, in its own commit (`ffac618`). It is one column at the player pages' width under "{season} · Admin", with the course and the tee each full width and rating and slope side by side. The front and back nine are stacked, headed "Front nine" and "Back nine", one 44 px row per hole, with Save tee across the bottom. Routes, inputs and the par script are unchanged.
- Add a player at `/admin/players`, built from `admin-player.html`. Add and make a link trims the name at the ends, keeps its case, stores the player through `create_player`, and opens their page at `/admin/players/link?player=…`. That page shows their link as text, the full URL they open, and New link, stop the old one, which calls `replace_link_token`.
- The roster lists every stored player in id order, under "Players". Each row is at least 44 px tall and opens that player's page.
- `create_player` refuses an empty name ("a player has a name") and a stored one ("player {id} is already stored") with ValueError. A refused add answers 400 with the name as typed and the refusal in a warn card above the form, and stores nothing. The page of a player nobody stored, and a new link for one, read "No such player." on the notice, 404.
- `scripts/demo.py shots admin <path>...` signs in through the demo admin link, so the admin screens are checked at 390 px.
- At your click-through: the course rating takes a comma as its decimal mark as well as a dot, so "71,8" is 718, while "71,85", "71,8,0" and "71,8.0" stay refused. The tee screen's hole rows are about 36 px, a 32 px input in 2 px of row padding. Both admin screens have a bottom nav, Players and Courses, with their own tab marked.
