# S17a Admin link and tees

**Implemented**

- The admin's own link (D19): `/admin/k/<token>` sets an HttpOnly admin cookie when the token is the deployment's `LEAGUE_ADMIN_TOKEN`, compared as bytes with `secrets.compare_digest`. Every admin route checks the cookie before it loads or stores anything, and a refused visit reads "Open your admin link." on the notice, 401.
- Add a course and tee at `/admin/tees`, from `docs/design/admin-tee.html`. Save tee stores the tee and opens its page at `/admin/tees/change?course=…&tee=…`, headed "Change a tee", with course and tee read-only, its numbers, "Stroke index uses 1 to 18 once each" and its par. Save tee there changes the numbers while R14 allows. A tee nobody stored reads "No such tee.", 404.
- The admin's error state: a refused save answers 400 with every field as typed and the refusal in place of the design's note, so the screen keeps one warn card. A ValueError reads in the domain's words, and a field that is no number reads the form's own sentence, the rating's first.
- The screen follows the redrawn `admin-tee.html`: course full width, then tee, rating and slope; front and back nine side by side, one row per hole; Save tee at the bottom. The design's par on each nine is left out (see Deviations). Its script moves a par to the next hole's par after one digit and selects a par's text on focus.
- `create_tee` refuses a stored course and tee name, `change_tee`, `create_round` and `change_round` refuse a tee nobody stored, and `Tee` refuses an empty course or tee name, a course rating outside 50.0 to 90.0 and a slope outside 55 to 155, all with ValueError.

**Deviations from plan**

- The owner's changes at approval: the refusal takes the note's place rather than sitting above it, and the notice helper and cookie lifetime live in the new `web/shared.py` rather than as private copies in `admin.py`. That replaces architect ruling 12.
- Architect ruling 11 asked for the `stored` helper in `tests/services/test_setup.py` to return `Tee | None`. Two callers build a `RoundFacts` from it, which needs a `Tee`, so the helper asserts the tee is there and returns `Tee`. The None case is tested through `stored_tee` instead.
- `test_load_tee_reads_none_for_a_tee_nobody_stored` uses T3's course with tee name "Red" for the course-only case, where ruling 10(g) named "Old Links", "Blue". Both share only the course with a stored tee.
- The refusal cases in ruling 10 and the plan are one parametrized test, which adds a form with both a bad rating and a bad slope (the rating's sentence wins) and a blank course. A separate test covers a missing field.
- The admin-link refusal test also covers a player's link token opened as an admin link.
- The domain, store and service edits were written before their tests, not after. Every one has a test now.
- From the owner after the audit: R13 bounds a tee's course rating at 50.0 to 90.0 and its slope at 55 to 155, in `Tee`, so a huge or zero slope reads a refusal instead of a 500; a refused change shows no par and no stroke-index line; the screen is rebuilt from the redrawn `admin-tee.html` with its par script, recorded in ARCHITECTURE's Stack and the design README; and BUILD_PLAN gains the course catalog among the later steps. The redrawn design's par on each nine ("Par 36") is left out: templates do no arithmetic, so it needs two properties on `Tee`, and with the rating and slope bounds `facts.py` would reach 310 lines, past the 300 limit. Showing it needs `Tee` moved to a module of its own first, a file outside this step's plan. The stored tee's page keeps the total Par.
- From the audit: the rating's read catches any decimal error, the test helper reads through `stored_tee`, `_page_of` is inlined and `_shown` sits above its caller.
- The design's `<form>` has two `style` attributes, of which a browser reads the first; the template keeps that one. The design's "read from the photo" line is left out with Fill from a photo (S19).

- The par script was checked in headless Chromium against the demo, since the test client runs no script: nine single digits typed from hole 1's par leave focus on hole 10's par, a digit in a stroke index leaves focus there, a par's text is selected when it takes focus, and a digit on hole 18's par stays there.

**Audit**

The reviewer made 9 findings and read the code without running it. The architect ruled on each.
1. A rating such as "1e999999" answered 500: applied, `_tenths` catches `DecimalException`, with a test. The missing bounds on rating and slope: sent to the owner, who chose R13 bounds of 50.0 to 90.0 and 55 to 155.
2. `Decimal` and `int` read "7.18e1", "+129", " 129 " and underscores: declined. Section 7 refuses a field that is no number, these are numbers, and the stored tee's page shows what was read.
3. The "no tee" guard appears twice in `setup.py`: declined. Each guard is two lines, a helper would move code without reducing it, and both messages are pinned by tests.
4. A refused change drops the par and check line: sent to the owner, who kept it, with SPEC's row saying so and a test asserting it. The Add screen shows no stroke-index line: declined, settled by a SPEC edit saying only a stored tee's page shows it.
5. The test helper `stored` duplicated `stored_tee`: applied.
6. `_page_of` and `_shown` were not directly above their one caller: applied, `_page_of` inlined and `_shown` moved.
7. S15d1's Accept said the keys refuse a duplicate tee and a round on an unknown one: applied as a BUILD_PLAN edit saying the use cases check first.
8. This report said every admin route checks the cookie "before it reads or writes": applied, now "before it loads or stores anything".
9. The notice's Home leads an admin without a player cookie to the player notice: declined, since SPEC's Notice row settles it.
