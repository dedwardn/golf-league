# T1 Competitors

**Implemented**

Whoever earns an award or holds a standings row is now named a competitor (R15) rather than a
player: `NetEntry.competitor_id`, `Side.competitor_ids`, `Award.competitor_id`,
`StandingsRow.competitor_id`, `ManualAward.competitor_id`, and the `competitor_ids` that
`standings` gives a row each. No behaviour changed: the suite passes with no test added, renamed
or given a new expected value. Cards, pairings, seed indexes and season teams stay about players.

`src/league/domain/season.py` needed no change: it passes the player id to `NetEntry` and the
ids to `standings` by position. 152 tests, 100 percent coverage of `league.domain`.

The diff with `competitor` mapped back to `player` and whitespace removed matches HEAD in every
file except for the formatter's one trailing comma in `pairing.py`, the `seeded_` prefix
dropped from the `standings` parameter, and the `allplay_awards` docstring below. Also edited
at `/finish`: `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md` (status).

Both answers to the plan's questions were approved as recommended: `standings` takes
`competitor_ids`, not `seeded_competitor_ids`, because a seed index belongs to a player, not a
team; and `virtual_side` takes `opponent_competitor_ids`, because it is checked against
`entry.competitor_id` and one thing should have one name.

*Settled for T2 and T3 (audit).* A `NetEntry` is what a contest ranks, so its `competitor_id`
stays. In a team season T2 and T3 build each team's score as a `NetEntry` whose competitor is
the team; a player's own net card in an own-ball round stays a `NetCard` and never becomes a
`NetEntry`. The code that reads the field as a player id (`_declared_side`,
`teambonus_awards`, `season._entries`) runs only in an individual season, where the competitor
is the player.

**Public API**

- `NetEntry(card_id: str, competitor_id: str, submitted_at: datetime, net: NetCard)`
- `Side(competitor_ids: tuple[str, ...], net: NetCard)`
- `Award(competitor_id: str, key: str, points: Fraction, reason: Reason)`
- `StandingsRow(position: int, competitor_id: str, points: Fraction)`
- `ManualAward(competitor_id: str, key: str, points: Fraction)`
- `standings(rounds: Sequence[RoundAwards], competitor_ids: Sequence[str], rule: StandingsRule, segment: Segment | None = None) -> tuple[StandingsRow, ...]`
- `virtual_side(references: Sequence[NetEntry], opponent_competitor_ids: Sequence[str], tee: Tee, virtual_n: int) -> Side`

No public name was added or removed, so the module map in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` is unchanged.

**Deviations from plan**

1. `tests/domain/test_contests_teambonus.py` was not in the plan's test list. Its `duel_award`
   helper builds an `Award` positionally, so nothing forced the change; its parameter was
   renamed to `competitor_id` to match the field it fills, as the `test_standings.py` helpers
   were.
2. The plan named docstring changes for `results.py`, `facts.py` and `standings.py` only. The
   docstrings of `single_side`, `field_awards` and `allplay_awards` also say competitor now,
   since `single_side` builds a `Side` for whoever holds the entry, and field and allplay will
   run over teams in T3. Duel docstrings, `_side_awards` among them, keep player, as a duel
   exists only in an individual season.
3. The `allplay_awards` docstring went two characters over the line limit with the rename. It
   now reads "Each on-time competitor's record (R9), in entry order.": the formula it restated
   is the code two lines below, and R9 already names it.

**Deliberately not done**

- `SeasonFacts.mode`, `RoundFacts.format`, team cards and the team branch of `compute_season`: T3.
- Team playing handicaps and team scores: T2.
- A team season's round winners by highest round total: T3.
- Test names that say player, such as `test_r10_a_seeded_player_who_has_not_played_has_a_row_at_zero`:
  in an individual season the competitor is the player, so they stay true.

**Open questions**

None.

**Audit**

1. `NetEntry.competitor_id` can hold a player id where SPEC's competitor is a team. Kept, and
   settled for T2 and T3 as recorded under Where.
2. Restore `virtual_side`'s `opponent_player_ids`, as its values come from a pairing. Declined:
   the owner chose `opponent_competitor_ids` in answer to the plan's second question, so it is
   approved in the plan, not a deviation.
3. `_side_awards`' docstring said competitors while its duel neighbours said players, against
   this report's own reasoning. Applied: it says players again.
4. The `allplay_awards` docstring restated the formula of the code below it. Applied: the
   line and the blank line before it are gone, which leaves a one-line docstring.
