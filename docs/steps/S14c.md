# S14c Services for admin: correct a round

**Implemented**

- `add_manual_award` stores an award on an open or closed round, after its earlier ones. A competitor who is not one of the season, a draft round and a missing round are refused (R9).
- `correct_card` replaces the hole scores of a counted card or team card and keeps its `submitted_at` and holder (R13).
- `void_card` keeps the row, no longer counted, and the holder may hand in a new card if the round still takes cards (R13).
- The tests' hand-written `void` is gone. Store tests call `update_card_voided`, and service tests call `void_card`.
