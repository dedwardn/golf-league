# S16c1 Round result

**Implemented**

- **The round result.** In a team season, `/rounds/{number}` shows the result to a viewer who sees the round (R12). That replaces S16b's "No such round." for them. A viewer from whom R12 hides an open round still reads Round before playing.
- **The screen, top to bottom.**
  - The viewer's team and format, and the team net with each hole counted no worse than net double bogey.
  - Each player's gross and strokes, or the team card's.
  - "Against everyone": "N of M teams beaten", the teams with a score ranked, and the teams without one named. With fewer than two teams with a score, the block reads "No other team has a score yet."
  - The round's points, then the change in position with the gap to the place above.
- **Where the numbers come from.** Every score and record on the page is `compute_season`'s:
  - `RoundView` now carries each team's stableford score and the round's allplay records, and the page reads them without recomputing anything;
  - the cap lives in `strokes.py`, beside the stableford rule;
  - the record lives in `contests.py`, where the allplay awards now scale from it.
- **Submit card** now lands on `/rounds/{number}`, so a player who has just handed in reads the result.

**Public API**

```python
# domain/results.py
class Record:      # competitor_id: str, beaten: Fraction
class RoundView:   # awards, winners, winning_total, scores: tuple[NetEntry, ...], records: tuple[Record, ...]

    def round(self, number: int) -> RoundView

# domain/contests.py
def allplay_records(contest: ContestEntries, basis: Basis) -> tuple[Record, ...]

# domain/strokes.py
def capped_net(net: NetCard, tee: Tee) -> NetCard

# domain/teams.py
def team_handicaps(index_tenths: tuple[int, int], tee: Tee, config: FormatConfig) -> tuple[int, ...]
def capped_team_net(score: NetCard, config: FormatConfig, tee: Tee) -> int

# domain/standings.py
def round_total(competitor_id: str, round_awards: RoundAwards) -> Fraction | None

# domain/round_result.py
class ResultPlayer:   # player_id: str, gross: int | None, playing_handicap: int
class ResultTeam:     # team_id: str, format: str, net: int | None, players: tuple[ResultPlayer, ...],
                      # team_gross: int | None, team_playing_handicap: int | None
class RankedTeam:     # team_id: str, net: int, beaten: Fraction, bar_percent: int, mine: bool
class ResultAllplay:  # ranked: tuple[RankedTeam, ...], without_score: tuple[str, ...];
                      # others -> int, beaten_text -> str | None, without_score_text -> str | None
class RoundResult:    # number: int, course: str, tee_name: str, team: ResultTeam | None,
                      # allplay: ResultAllplay, points: Fraction | None,

def round_result(
    facts: SeasonFacts, view: SeasonView, number: int, viewer_id: str, now: datetime

# services/play.py (replaces locked_round_view)
def round_page_view(
    engine: Engine, season_id: str, number: int, viewer_id: str, now: datetime

```

**Deviations from plan**

1. **The scores, records and cap moved out of `round_result.py` at the audit.** This follows the owner's direction and the architect's rulings.
   - The plan rejected reusing `team_rounds.py`'s scores and kept the cap private. The audit reversed both.
   - Six modules outside the approved plan changed for it: `contests.py`, `strokes.py`, `teams.py`, `team_rounds.py`, `season.py`, and `home.py` beyond its lookup. BUILD_PLAN's module list now names them.
2. **`before` and `after` are the team's `StandingsRow`s,** not ordinal strings with a separate `gap`. A row already carries `position_text` and its `gap`, as `HomeView.row` does.
3. **`beaten` and `bar_percent` sit on each `RankedTeam`.** `others`, `beaten_text` and `without_score_text` are properties (ruling 11 at the plan).
4. **Two fields are read in a domain test only so vulture sees them.** They are `ResultTeam.team_gross` and `RankedTeam.bar_percent`, read in ann's open round 3 case, which decides both (S16b's precedent; kept at the audit).
5. **Domain cases the plan did not name:**
   - cam on closed round 3, a shared-ball team without its team card;
   - from the audit, a lone team, and a combined net on a total-basis allplay.
6. **The net column is 30 px wide where the design draws 24.** A combined net has three digits.

**Open questions**

- **A team-season allplay on total, where a cap bites.** Settled by the owner after the audit: leave it, since every 2026-27 round is on stableford (decision 6). "N of M" and the ranking follow the comparison on total, while the net column is capped (section 7), so where a cap bites a capped 69 could be listed below a 70.
- **A round with field contests only,** or with no allplay, reads "No other team has a score yet." since it has no records. Decision 6 gives every round an allplay contest.
- **With fewer than two teams with a score,** a viewer whose team has none also reads "No other team has a score yet."
- **"have" after a single team,** as in "green have no score this round.", follows the design's "Petter & Åsmund have". It reads oddly with T3's one-word ids.
