# S17d2a Rounds: the season's rounds

**Implemented**

- The Rounds tab, `/admin/rounds`, lists every round of the season, drafts included, in number order. Each row reads "Round {n} · {format}", its course and tee name, and a Draft, Open or Closed pill, and opens `/admin/rounds/{n}`.
- The round screen is `admin-round.html`'s header, whose back arrow opens Rounds, and its round card read as text: the course, tee and par; Opens and Closes in Oslo time; the format, and under it, full width, one "Handicap allowance" line filled from the round's own numbers, such as "85% each" or "35% lower, 15% higher". An open or closed round adds "A round's setup is fixed once it opens."
- A round without a format, in an individual season, reads "Round {n}" alone. A round the season does not have reads "No such round." (404). Both routes refuse a visit without the admin cookie.
- Rounds is in the admin nav of every admin screen, second, and in Season setup's topbar. The nav is now one included template, `admin-nav.html`.

**Public API**

- `league.domain.facts.StoredRound(round: RoundFacts, state: str, opens_at: datetime, closes_at: datetime)`
- `league.store.load.load_stored_rounds(connection: Connection, season_id: str) -> tuple[StoredRound, ...]`
- `league.services.setup.stored_rounds(engine: Engine, season_id: str) -> tuple[StoredRound, ...]`

- `league.web.admin_round.round_router() -> APIRouter`: GET `/admin/rounds` and GET `/admin/rounds/{number}`.

**Deviations from plan**

- **The allowance is one "Handicap allowance" line**, not the plan's labels per allowance ("Strokes, % each", "Lower handicap, %" and the rest). The owner changed this at the click-through, where "Strokes, % of the pair's total" wrapped over two lines and scramble's second number sat alone on its own row.
- **The services test is parametrized over the existing `NEW_ROUNDS`**, so it covers an individual draft and a team draft rather than one draft.
- **The web tests build their own season with no round** (`replace(T3, rounds=())`), with `t3_open`, in a module template. They also add a `database` fixture, so a test can create a draft on the engine directly, and a `browse` fixture, a copy of `test_app`'s `browser`, since an imported fixture fails the lint (F401 and F811) and `noqa` is forbidden.

  - Add a round and the new round's number

- Deleting a draft round belongs to no step.

**The audit**

The reviewer raised seven findings, and the architect ruled on them:
- **1, applied.** The allowance labels moved from `_allowances` in `admin_round.py` into `formats.html`, beside each format's name, line and game type. The round card prints `round.format | attr(field)`. `_allowances`, its alias and the configs import are gone, and no test changed.
- **2, declined.** The template only picks a printed line from the stored state, as the list's pill does, and decides no write. A domain predicate would have one reader, a template. Whether the tee, format and window lock when a round opens is S17d2b's open question, which BUILD_PLAN now widens to whether R14 or SPEC's row is reworded and whether the form and `change_round` then read one predicate in `access.py`.
- **3, declined.** Importing `test_app`'s `browser` fixture fails ruff's F401 and F811, which only `noqa` would silence. Each test module owns its fixtures, as in eight other files.
- **4, declined.** A macro for the one-line heading adds a definition and two imports and removes nothing.
- **5, declined, with a doc edit.** The plan review ruled `StoredRound` without a check of its own, since no rule reads its state and the store's CHECK holds it. ARCHITECTURE's store map now says the CHECK holds what no dataclass checks.
- **6, declined.** `RoundHeading` serves home, which needs open rounds only and no cards.
- **7, declined.** Picking a round from the season's rounds matches `_player_page`, and `load_round` needs a state.

**The owner's click-through**

On the phone, every step was OK, and "A round's setup is fixed once it opens." stays. The owner changed one thing: the allowance labels read clumsily and wrapped. The card now shows the format and, under it, full width, "Handicap allowance": "85% each", "100% each", "50% of the pair's total", "35% lower, 15% higher" or "60% lower, 40% higher", from the round's own numbers. These lines are kept in `formats.html` beside the format's name. The tests now assert each line, including a draft's "40% lower, 10% higher". The labels of the number inputs are S17d2b's.
