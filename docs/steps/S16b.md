# S16b Round before playing

**Implemented**

- **The round before playing.** In a team season, `/rounds/{number}` shows an open round to a viewer from whom R12 hides it. It shows the round's number and its close in local time, then "Teams with a score" as "3 of 4", then one row per season team in team id order. Each row reads "Score in", "1 of 2 cards" or "Not yet", and the viewer's team is marked.
- **Register card and the help line.** While the round takes the viewer's card, the screen shows the help line "Scores stay hidden until your card is in, so nobody plays knowing the number they need." and Register card, which opens S16a's Check the card. A viewer outside the season reads the rows without either.
- **Who reads "No such round."** A viewer who sees the round, because their card or their team's team card is in, reads "No such round." until S16c, as does anyone asking for a round that is not open.
- **Where the rows come from.** "Score in" reads the teams with a score from `compute_season`'s `card_holders`, as home does. "1 of 2 cards" reads `holds_card` for each of a team's two players.

**Deviations from plan**

- **One status in place of two flags.** At approval, the owner replaced `LockedTeam`'s `scored` and `one_of_two` booleans with one `status`, so no team can be built as both. The template maps each status to its pill. The `Literal` alias is private to `locked.py`.
- **ivy's domain test alone reads `takes_card` beside `with_score`.** Vulture reports a field that only a template reads as unused, and ivy's test is the one that decides that field's value, as in the precedent of `bay.shared_ball`.
- **The web 404s are one parametrized test.** It covers a viewer who sees the round, closed round 2 and absent round 6. The closed-round case asks as cam, as above.
