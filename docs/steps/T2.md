# T2 Team handicaps and team scores

**Implemented**

The round's scoring config now lives in `domain/configs.py`, moved out of `facts.py` in its own
commit. Each team format has a config there with R16's default allowances and their bounds. R1's
bracket is the public `course_handicap`, which `playing_handicap` rounds. `teams.py` gives a
shared-ball team's playing handicap and an own-ball team's score on a basis (R17). A team card is
a `Card`.

**Audit**

1. Best ball's per-hole minimum is written in both `sides.py` (`bestball_side`, R7) and
   `teams.py` (`own_ball_score`, R17). Kept as two. R7 reads entries and leaves out a member
   without a card; R17 reads two net cards and has no score without both. An own-ball net card
   never becomes a `NetEntry` (settled at T1), so `teams.py` cannot call `bestball_side`, and a
   shared public function would add a name, a definition and a docstring to save one expression.
2. Combined on stableford inverts R3's points formula (`par + 2 - x - y`). No change: BUILD_PLAN
   T2 settles the synthetic net card, the `own_ball_score` docstring says why, and
   `test_r17_combined_on_stableford_sums_the_points_not_the_points_of_the_summed_net` fails if
   R3's formula and the inversion part.
3. Fold foursome into scramble's expression with equal weights. Declined: it saves one line and
   reads foursome as a weighted scramble, where R16 writes it as its own formula, which the code
   mirrors.
4. The `strokes.py` module docstring repeated `course_handicap`'s. Cut to "R1, R2 and R3, and
   R16's course handicap."
5. `course handicap` and `team playing handicap` are public names the glossary does not list.
   Applied by the owner, who added both rows to SPEC's glossary in a commit of its own; a finding
   about `docs/` is reported and never applied by the builder (CLAUDE.md). A team card held in a `Card` is not a
   contradiction: the glossary names the concept, and BUILD_PLAN T2 and ARCHITECTURE's
   `facts.py` row settle the type (`2f9b3a8`).
6. Two tests compose `strokes.py` functions with no team code of their own: the best-ball half of
   `test_r16_an_allowance_overridden_by_the_round`, and
   `test_r17_a_shared_ball_team_card_is_netted_at_the_team_playing_handicap`. Kept. R16's own-ball
   handicap is R1 at the format's allowance, which is `playing_handicap`, and BUILD_PLAN T2 makes a
   shared-ball team's score R3's `net_card` with no wrapper. Both are accept lines.
