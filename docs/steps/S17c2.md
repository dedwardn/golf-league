# S17c2 Correct a season

**Implemented**

- **Correct a league index.** In a team season, while no round is closed, each player row of Season setup's read-back reads Player, League index, an empty Golfbox input and Correct, under the fine line "To correct a league index, type the player's Golfbox handicap. The index is 70% of it." Each row is its own form, so Enter in a row corrects that row. The typed Golfbox handicap is read as Save season reads one (trimmed, "+1.2" a plus handicap, a comma as decimal mark), turned into a seed index at the seed share and stored through `correct_seed_index`. A refused correction answers 400 with that row as typed and one warn card. A correction posted to an individual season opens Season setup and stores nothing.
- **Once a round is closed** the correction leaves the screen, and the Players card reads "League indexes are fixed once a round is closed.", which is now also `correct_seed_index`'s refusal. An individual season's read-back has no correction.
- **Bay settings change on the read-back** at any time, in either season mode, through `change_bay_settings`. The card is the create form's bay card with Save bay settings below Add a line. Add a line, × and Enter (an invisible first submit button named `redraw`) redraw with the lines as typed and store nothing.
- **No team in a player row.** The read-back's player rows have no Team column in any state, since the Teams card shows the teams. The locked and the individual read-back's rows read Player and League index.
- **Phone width.** At 390 px the correctable read-back has a scrollWidth equal to its clientWidth of 390, before and after a refused correction (checked in Chromium through Playwright). The Players card's header and rows share one column template, `minmax(0,1fr) 88px 64px 76px` when correctable and `minmax(0,1fr) 88px` otherwise. Every column after Player has a fixed width, so Player is as wide in the header as in each row and each header cell starts at its rows' x. An `auto` column would size to its own grid's content, and the empty header cell over Correct would be 0 wide. No header wraps or clips (measured: "League index" is 88 px wide at its natural width).
